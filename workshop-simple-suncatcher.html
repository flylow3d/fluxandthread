Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience?

Not at all — this is a true beginner class. Sarah walks you through every step, from your very first cut to soldering the finished piece. All skill levels are welcome.

Do I get to choose my own glass?

Yes — and it’s everyone’s favorite part! When you book (or a few days before class), you’ll hand-pick the exact glass colors for each piece of your suncatcher from our collection of beautiful sheets — reserved just for you. Prefer a surprise? Sarah is happy to hand-pick the perfect glass for you.

How old do I need to be?

This workshop is for ages 18 and up.

Can I come with a friend?

Please do — it’s a lovely thing to do together! Just reserve a seat for each of you, since spots are limited to four per class. Book early so you can sit side by side.

How does payment work?

It’s $60 per seat, paid in full to reserve. Just click Check for an Open Seat to book and pay securely through Square — your seat is locked the moment you check out.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Plans change! If you cancel 5 or more days before class, you’ll receive a 75% refund — or you can transfer your seat to a friend or a future class. Within 5 days of class, payment is non-refundable, but you’re still welcome to send a friend in your place or move to a future class.

What should I bring?

Just closed-toe shoes (required) and a hair tie if your hair is long. A water bottle and a snack are nice to have. Everything else — glass, tools, safety glasses, and aprons — is provided.

Where is it?

We’re at Well Grounded Cafe, 14517 Lima Rd, Fort Wayne, IN 46818 (get directions). Come a few minutes early and we’ll get you set up!

What if the class is full?

Join the waitlist on this page and we’ll email you the moment a seat opens up — or as soon as the next date is announced.
